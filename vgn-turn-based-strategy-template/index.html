Turn-Based Strategy Template

A heroes-style template — overland hex map, towns, heroes, and tactical combat

How to play

  • Click your hero, then click a hex to move. Visit resource piles, mines and dwellings on the way.
  • Enter your town to build structures and recruit creatures.
  • Click wandering monsters, enemy heroes and enemy towns to attack them.
  • Capture every enemy town and defeat every enemy hero to win.
